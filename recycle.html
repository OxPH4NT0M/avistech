Operational Standards

How Avistech handles every consignment.

Structured around traceable logistics, disciplined mechanical segregation, and full statutory compliance under India's E-Waste and Battery Waste Management Rules.

01

High-Yield Material Recovery

Systematic separation of ferrous metals, copper, aluminum, engineered plastics, and battery-grade fractions to maximize circular value and cut virgin resource extraction.

02

Environmentally Sound Technology

Controlled mechanical dismantling and safe chemical containment designed to keep heavy metals, acid electrolytes, and toxic particulates out of soil and groundwater.

03

Traceable Collection Network

Scheduled reverse logistics directly from offices, factories, data centers, and institutional campuses with weighed manifests and tamper-evident handover records.

04

Closed-Loop Zero-Landfill Process

End-of-life assets are routed through audited downstream refining rather than informal scrap yards or landfills, protecting both your ESG goals and brand liability.

05

Complete Statutory & EPR Compliance

Full alignment with Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB) frameworks, including Form-6 manifests and destruction certificates.
